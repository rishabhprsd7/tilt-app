Design a mobile app called “Tilt” (working title).

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT an expense tracker.
This is NOT Splitwise.
This is NOT fintech.

This is a playful social app for generous people who want emotional fairness without transactional accounting.

CORE IDEA:

People often overspend in relationships, friendships, trips, and social groups because they are generous.

Most expense apps create pressure because they track exact money.

This app intentionally removes precision.

Users NEVER enter exact monetary values.

Instead, users select predefined “treat levels” like:
+10
+20
+30
+50
+75
+100
+130
+150
+180
+200
+225
+250
+280
+300

These values are intentionally uneven and psychologically fuzzy.

The app should feel symbolic and social, not mathematical.

EXAMPLE:

If someone buys dinner for friends:
they do NOT enter €43.

They simply choose:
+50 🍝

Another friend later buys coffee:
+10 ☕

Another buys drinks:
+20 🍻

Over time the social balance naturally tilts back and forth.

BALANCE SYSTEM:

NEVER show:

* debt
* repayment
* negative balances
* settlements
* “you owe”

Instead use language like:

* “Tilted toward Alex”
* “Jamie has been carrying the vibe lately”
* “Your turn to spoil the squad 🍕”
* “Chris has entered generous goblin mode”
* “Balance vibes are returning ✨”

IMPORTANT:
The app should encourage generosity and playfulness, not guilt.

GROUP MECHANIC:

Groups are a core feature.

Example:
Group members:
A, B, C, D, E

A logs:
+100 🍣

But selects only:
B and C

Result:

* A gains social balance only against B and C
* D and E are unaffected

This creates lightweight social reciprocity without complicated debt splitting.

The app should intelligently divide the symbolic value among selected participants.

NO EXACT MATH SHOULD EVER BE EXPOSED TO USERS.
